## Model Orchestration
When running on Fable for codebase-heavy or token-heavy work, invoke the
`efficient-fable` skill and follow it: keep Fable on planning, architecture,
synthesis, conflict-resolution, and final review, and delegate research,
implementation, testing, and computer-use to cheaper Opus 4.8 subagents
(set the subagent `model` override). Do this without being asked.
